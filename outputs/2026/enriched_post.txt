The annual CVE record now falls before most teams finish a roadmap.

September closed at 14,943 published CVEs, +245.8% against September 2025, which puts 2026 at 72,812 year to date (+105.9% year over year) and 267 CVEs published a day so far this year.

Some of that is who is publishing rather than what is breaking: the five busiest assigners supplied 7,063 of September's CVEs, 47.3%, and September 8 alone carried 1,559, 965 of those from Microsoft.

Median CVSS v3.x was 7.3 with the 75th percentile at 8.1, across the 12,231 of 14,943 CVEs carrying a v3.x score. Another 1,266 carry only a v4.0 score and 1,446 carry none.

Most frequently assigned weaknesses, 14,718 assignments across 12,754 CVEs since a CVE can carry more than one:

  Missing Authorization (CWE-862): 797
  Improper Access Control (CWE-284): 610
  SQL Injection (CWE-89): 494
  Use After Free (CWE-416): 469
