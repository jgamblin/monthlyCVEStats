The annual CVE record now falls before most teams finish a roadmap.

September 2026 closed at 14,943 published CVEs against 4,321 in September 2025, +245.8%.

That puts 2026 at 72,812 CVEs year to date, +105.9% year over year, and 267 CVEs published a day so far this year. Some of that is who is publishing rather than what is breaking: the five busiest assigners supplied 7,063 of September's CVEs, 47.3%, and September 8 alone carried 1,559, 965 of those from Microsoft. 2026 has now published more CVEs than the whole of 2025 (48,152), and is 24,660 ahead with three months left to count.

How far ahead can you plan when the baseline moves every year?

Source: NVD, excluding rejected CVEs
