Beating last year's total stopped being news once it happened in summer.

October 2026 closed at 17,060 published CVEs against 4,273 in October 2025, +299.3%.

That puts 2026 at 89,859 CVEs year to date, +126.7% year over year, and 296 CVEs published a day so far this year. Some of that is who is publishing rather than what is breaking: the five busiest assigners supplied 7,638 of October's CVEs, 44.8%, and October 13 alone carried 2,303, 1,407 of those from Microsoft. 2026 has now published more CVEs than the whole of 2025 (48,151), and is 41,708 ahead with two months left to count.

What breaks first when a record year becomes an average one?

Source: NVD, excluding rejected CVEs
