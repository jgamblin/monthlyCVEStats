This is not a spike year, it is the floor the next one starts from.

July 2026 closed at 9,775 published CVEs against 3,776 in July 2025, +158.9%.

That puts 2026 at 45,626 CVEs year to date, +66.4% year over year, and 215 CVEs published a day so far this year. July 21 alone carried 1,474 of them, 1,097 of those from Oracle. All of 2025 came to 48,162. 2026 passes it in another 2,536 CVEs, in the second week of August.
